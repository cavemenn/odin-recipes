Matcha Cloud Foam

Ingredients:

  • Whipping Cream: 45-50g
  • Full Cream Milk: 15g
  • Vanilla Syrup: 5g

Instructions:

  1. Pour 45-50g of Whipping Cream into a pitcher/cup
  2. Pour 15g of Full Cream Milk into the same pitcher
  3. Pour 5g of Vanilla Syrup into the mixture
  4. Whisk with a milk frother on high speed first then go to low
  5. Pour on a glass of milk and enjoy
